# Native platform folders

The flavor setup is finished and frozen: Gradle product flavors, Xcode Dev/Staging/Prod schemes, `.vscode/launch.json` and `package_rename_config.yaml` all agree on `dev`, `staging` and `prod` with entry points `lib/main_dev.dart`, `lib/main_staging.dart`, `lib/main_prod.dart`. Do not change any of it as a side effect of Dart work. Run commands are in `README.md`.

iOS and macOS use Swift Package Manager, not CocoaPods.

Constraints from the dependency set:

- `flutter_secure_storage` 11 requires Android `minSdk` 24 and `compileSdk` 37.
- `sentry_flutter` needs no per-flavor native files; the DSN is a `--dart-define`.

Files under `*/flutter/generated_plugin*` and `GeneratedPluginRegistrant.*` are regenerated by `flutter pub get`; never edit them.
