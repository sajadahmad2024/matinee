# P2P games — end-to-end UI

**Type:** feat

The P2P tab and the three games behind it, from the six Figma sections the request
names. The Home frames those sections repeat are out of scope.

## Promotions

Second use, so these move to `core/widgets` rather than being copied:

- `RedeemCard` and `CategoryTag` from `features/rewards` — the hub's game cards
  are the catalogue's `gameCard`, the same component the rewards list uses.
- `StatusBadge` from `features/earns` — quests, curated rows and predictions all
  carry it.

New shared primitives, both specified across features by the catalogue:
`ProgressRing` (`progress.ring`) and `CheckDisc` (`checkDisc`).

## Data decisions

- The balance and the badge ladder come from `shared/points`, so the hub agrees
  with Rewards and My Earns rather than repeating the frame's own figures.
- A quest's progress is its action count, one number for the hero card's
  fraction, the ring and the "n of m actions done" line.
- Percentages and remainders are derived: `noPercent`, `minutesLeft`,
  `daysToNextLevel` and every progress fraction come off a stored value.
